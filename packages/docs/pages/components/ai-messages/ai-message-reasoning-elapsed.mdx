# AIMessageReasoningElapsed

<ComponentDescription>The time since the reasoning started, such as "40s" and then "1m 5s". It refreshes every second and renders only while the reasoning runs.</ComponentDescription>

```tsx
<AIMessageReasoningHeader>
  <AIMessageReasoningLabel />
  <AIMessageReasoningElapsed />
</AIMessageReasoningHeader>
```

The time counts from the `startedAt` of the panel (see [AIMessageReasoningRoot](/components/ai-messages/ai-message-reasoning-root)) and is formatted with `Intl` in the locale of the `LocaleProvider`: en-US "1m 5s", pt-BR "1 min 5 s". Only this piece re-renders every second.

## Props

`AIMessageReasoningElapsed` has no props of its own. It accepts the props of a `span`.

## Accessibility

The elapsed time is not a live region and stays outside the label, so screen readers do not announce every tick.

## Styling

| Attribute | Region |
|-----------|--------|
| `[data-sl-ai-message-reasoning-elapsed]` | The time, only while running, with tabular numbers |
