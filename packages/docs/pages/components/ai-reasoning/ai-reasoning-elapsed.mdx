# AIReasoningElapsed

<ComponentDescription>The time since the reasoning started, such as "40s" and then "1m 5s". It refreshes every second and renders only while the reasoning runs.</ComponentDescription>

```tsx
<AIReasoningHeader>
  <AIReasoningLabel />
  <AIReasoningElapsed />
</AIReasoningHeader>
```

The time counts from the `startedAt` of the panel (see [AIReasoningRoot](/components/ai-reasoning/ai-reasoning-root)) and is formatted with `Intl` in the locale of the `LocaleProvider` (en-US "1m 5s"). The pt-BR catalog overrides the unit format to match the design ("1m 5s" instead of "1 min 5 s"). Only this piece re-renders every second.

## Props

`AIReasoningElapsed` has no props of its own. It accepts the props of a `span`.

## Accessibility

The elapsed time is not a live region and stays outside the label, so screen readers do not announce every tick.

## Styling

| Attribute | Region |
|-----------|--------|
| `[data-sl-ai-reasoning-elapsed]` | The time, only while running, with tabular numbers |
