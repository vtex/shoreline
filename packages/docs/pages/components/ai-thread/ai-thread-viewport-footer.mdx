# AIThreadViewportFooter

<ComponentDescription>The footer that stays at the bottom of the viewport, where the composer goes. Its height is measured, so the last message is never hidden behind it.</ComponentDescription>

```tsx
<AIThreadViewport>
  <AIMessages />
  <AIThreadViewportFooter>
    <AIThreadScrollToBottom />
    <AIComposer />
  </AIThreadViewportFooter>
</AIThreadViewport>
```

It is sticky at the bottom of the viewport, and its `margin-top: auto` keeps it at the bottom of a short or empty thread. Put [AIThreadScrollToBottom](/components/ai-thread/ai-thread-scroll-to-bottom) first, then the composer, alerts or anything that must stay fixed.

## Props

`AIThreadViewportFooter` has no props of its own. It accepts the props of a `div`.

## Styling

| Attribute | Region |
|-----------|--------|
| `[data-sl-ai-thread-viewport-footer]` | The sticky footer, a flex column on the `--sl-bg-base` background. It has the thread padding on its sides (24px) and bottom (16px), and none on top |
