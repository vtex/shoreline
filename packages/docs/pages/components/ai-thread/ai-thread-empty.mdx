# AIThreadEmpty

<ComponentDescription>The welcome of the thread. It renders its children only while the thread has no messages and is not opening a thread, grows to fill the free space, and centers its children.</ComponentDescription>

```tsx
<AIThreadViewport>
  <AIThreadEmpty>
    <Text variant="display3">How can I help you today?</Text>
  </AIThreadEmpty>
  <AIMessages />
</AIThreadViewport>
```

It stays hidden while `useAIThread().isOpeningThread` is `true`, so the welcome does not flash before the history arrives. It has no other styles: the look of the welcome is yours. See the [empty thread example](/components/ai-thread#empty-thread).

## Props

`AIThreadEmpty` has no props of its own. It accepts the props of a `div`.

## Styling

| Attribute | Region |
|-----------|--------|
| `[data-sl-ai-thread-empty]` | The empty slot. It fills the free space (`flex: 1`) and centers its children |
