# AIThreadScrollToBottom

<ComponentDescription>The round button that takes people back to the latest message. It sits above the footer and only shows after the person scrolls up.</ComponentDescription>

```tsx
<AIThreadViewportFooter>
  <AIThreadScrollToBottom />
  <AIComposer />
</AIThreadViewportFooter>
```

Put it as the **first child** of [AIThreadViewportFooter](/components/ai-thread/ai-thread-viewport-footer). When the viewport is already at the bottom it is disabled and fully transparent. See the [scroll to bottom example](/components/ai-thread#scroll-to-bottom).

## Props

`AIThreadScrollToBottom` has no props of its own. It accepts the props of a `button`; its `children` replace the default arrow icon.

## Accessibility

Its accessible name is "Scroll to bottom" (`scrollToBottom`). Change it with `messages` on `AIThread`; see [Labels and languages](/components/ai-thread#labels-and-languages). While it is disabled it is out of the tab order.

## Styling

| Attribute | Region |
|-----------|--------|
| `[data-sl-ai-thread-scroll-to-bottom]` | The 28px round button with no border, centered 28px above the footer. Its shadow is `--sl-shadow-5` on Horizon and `--sl-shadow-1` on Sunrise. Its `:disabled` state is transparent and ignores the pointer |
